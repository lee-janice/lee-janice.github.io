The following recipe was adapted from this [Ginger chicken and rice soup with bok choy recipe](https://www.healthybyfork.com/ginger-chicken-and-rice-soup-with-bok-choy/).

# 🥫 Ingredients

## Broth base

- 2 tbsp avocado oil
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 thinly sliced yellow onion
- 1 inch piece of ginger, sliced into matchsticks
- Minced garlic

- 8 cups chicken broth
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1/2 tsp tumeric
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp black pepper

## Fillings

- 1 rotisserie chicken
- 3 cups chopped bok choy
- 1/2 cup uncooked jasmine rice

## Toppings

- Juice of half a lime
- Cilantro
- Green onions
- Lao Gan Ma chili oil

# 🔪 Instructions

1. Saute the onions, garlic, and ginger in the avocado and sesame oil until soft and fragrant.
2. Add in chicken broth, soy sauce, rice vinegar, tumeric, salt, and pepper.
3. Add jasmine rice. Cover and simmer 20 minutes.
4. While simmering, shred the rotisserie chicken. After rice is cooked through, add shredded chicken.
5. Add bok choy and cook 3-4 minutes.
6. Ladle into serving bowls and top with green onions, cilantro, chili oil, and lime juice to taste.
